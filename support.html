Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and enter your email; we send a reset link.

I bought Premium but it isn't active. Open the Premium screen (the gold crown at the top of the app) and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you paid with.

How do I delete my account? Profile › Account Settings › Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all progress. You can also email us to do it for you.

I don't get reminders. Turn on Daily Reminder in Profile, then allow notifications for DYP Imtahan in your device settings.

How do I change the language? Profile › Language: Azerbaijani, Russian or English. The questions switch too.

Does it work offline? Yes. Questions, exams and games work without internet; signing in, purchases and ads need a connection.
