About this policy

DYP Imtahan is a driving-exam practice app for Azerbaijan, published by AzCrew ("we", "us") on the Apple App Store and Google Play. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. It applies to the iOS and Android apps.

Questions: aliakhundzade@gmail.com.

How we use it

  • To run your account: sign you in, keep you signed in, send verification and password-reset emails, and show your name in the app.
  • To send notifications: campaign announcements, if you allow notifications. Study reminders are scheduled on your device and use no data.
  • To fix and improve the app: find crashes and see which features are used, in aggregate.
  • To show optional ads: rewarded ads you choose to watch, for example to unlock an extra exam attempt.
  • To tune the app remotely: settings such as quiz timers come from Firebase Remote Config.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Ads and tracking

The app shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch; there are no banners or forced ads, and Premium removes the need for them. On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission, so AdMob cannot use your device's advertising identifier and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

On Android, AdMob may use the device advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile › Account Settings › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account (email, name and user ID) from Firebase Authentication and erases all progress and settings stored on the device. It cannot be undone.

  • Sign in with Apple: deleting the account also revokes the app's access to your Apple Account. You can review this anytime in your iPhone Settings under Sign in with Apple.
  • Without the app: email aliakhundzade@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Analytics and crash data: not linked to your account, and deleted automatically under Firebase's retention periods — at most 14 months for analytics and 90 days for crash reports.

We keep account details only while your account exists.

Children, security and your rights

  • Children: the app is for people preparing for a driving exam and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
  • Security: data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store them.
  • Where data is processed: Google and Apple may process data on servers outside Azerbaijan, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.
  • Your rights: you can ask us what account information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes and contact

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date above. If a change affects how we use your account information, we will also tell you in the app.
