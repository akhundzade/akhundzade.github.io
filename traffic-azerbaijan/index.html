Frequently asked questions

I bought Premium but it isn't active. Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Alışları bərpa et (Restore purchases), signed in to the same Apple or Google account you paid with.

I paid for coins but didn't get them. Close and reopen the game with an internet connection; an unfinished purchase is credited then. If they still don't arrive, email us with the date of the purchase.

What language is the game in? Azerbaijani. Its rules and road signs follow Azerbaijan's Law on Road Traffic.

Does it work offline? Yes. The levels, the junction test and the sign album work without internet; ads and purchases need a connection.

I don't get reminders. Turn on Xatırlatma (Reminders) in Settings, then allow notifications for Traffic Azerbaijan in your phone's settings.

How do I change my ad choices? Settings › Gizlilik seçimləri (Privacy options) reopens the consent form. Ads in the game are optional rewarded videos; you never have to watch one.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device, and there is no account. Deleting the game deletes it.
