Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

About this policy

Traffic Azerbaijan is a puzzle game that teaches Azerbaijan's traffic rules, published by AzCrew ("we", "us") on Google Play and the Apple App Store. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it is shared with, and how it is deleted. It applies to the Android and iOS games.

Questions: aliakhundzade@gmail.com.

Information we collect

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. It collects only what it needs to keep working, to understand how it is played, and to pay for itself with optional ads and purchases.

Stored only on your device: your progress, stars, coins, hints, cars and their colours and plates, your licence and its penalty points, the sign album, quiz scores and settings. They are not uploaded to us, and they are erased when you delete the game. Premium is restored from your Google or Apple account, not from us.

How we use it

  • To make the game better: see, in aggregate, which levels are too hard and which features are used.
  • To show optional ads: rewarded ads you choose to watch, for example for more moves or a free hint. There are no banners or forced ads.
  • To deliver purchases: check each purchase with Google or Apple, credit it once, and restore Premium on a new phone.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Ads, consent and tracking

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized, using Google's consent form. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy options.

On iOS the game asks for Apple's tracking permission. If you say no, AdMob cannot use your device's advertising identifier and ads are not personalized; the game and its rewarded ads work the same either way.

On Android, AdMob may use the device advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings.

Premium gives the same rewards without watching ads.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: deleting the game erases everything it stored.
  • Analytics: not linked to you, and deleted automatically under Firebase's retention period, at most 14 months.
  • On request: email aliakhundzade@gmail.com and we will delete any analytics data we can find for your device within 30 days.

Children, security and your rights

  • Children: the game is for teenagers and adults learning the traffic rules and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information; if you believe a child's information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.
  • Security: data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and purchase receipts are checked against Google's and Apple's signatures.
  • Where data is processed: Google and Apple may process data on servers outside Azerbaijan, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.
  • Your rights: you can ask us what information we hold about you, have it deleted, or object to its use. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Not an official app

Traffic Azerbaijan is an independent game. It is not affiliated with the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the State Road Police or any other government body.
